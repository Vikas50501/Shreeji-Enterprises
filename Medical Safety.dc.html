Electrical safety for patients and staff

In a hospital, patient care comes first, and a power failure at the wrong moment can endanger treatment, diagnosis and life itself. As the number of mains-powered medical devices grows, so do the demands on the electrical installation, especially in operating theatres, cardiac catheterisation rooms and intensive care units.
