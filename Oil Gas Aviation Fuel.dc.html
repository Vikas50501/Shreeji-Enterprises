Aviation fuel hydrant tightness monitoring

Airports use hydrant systems to refuel aircraft, and any leak in them must be caught early. Our tightness monitoring solutions use a statistical test method that analyses pressure variation at higher and lower pressure to confirm the tightness of a test section, without needing temperature sensors. Leak location is provided by acquisition units.

Permanent online monitoring

Continuous tightness monitoring for hydrant systems.

Portable monitoring unit

Supplied in carry cases for quick connection and relocation between hydrant segments.

Leak calibration manifold

Portable manifold for accurate low-flow measurement in hazardous areas, used to verify the monitoring system.

All solutions are designed to meet recognised industry guidelines for aviation fuel systems.
